02

Brew it right

Our roastery recipes, dialed on the bar every morning. Pick your weapon.

1:16coffee to water
Medium-finegrind
94°Cwater
2:45total time
  1. Rinse the paper filter, preheat the brewer, discard the water.
  2. 15 g coffee · bloom with 45 g water for 35 seconds.
  3. Pour in slow spirals up to 240 g by 1:30.
  4. Let it draw down flat — swirl once, then hands off.
1:12coffee to water
Mediumgrind
90°Cwater
1:30total time
  1. Inverted method: 16 g coffee in, 190 g water in one pour.
  2. Stir three times, cap with a rinsed filter.
  3. At 1:10 flip onto a sturdy mug.
  4. Press for 20 seconds — stop at the first hiss.
1:15coffee to water
Coarsegrind
96°Cwater
4:00total time
  1. 30 g coffee, 450 g water, all at once. Lid on, don't plunge.
  2. At 4:00 break the crust and skim the foam.
  3. Rest another 4 minutes for the fines to settle.
  4. Plunge just below the surface and pour gently.
1:2coffee to beverage
Finegrind
93°Cwater
0:27shot time
  1. 18 g in, 36 g out — scale on, always.
  2. Distribute and tamp flat; no polishing theatrics.
  3. Pull at 9 bar; aim for a 26–28 second run.
  4. Sour? Grind finer. Bitter? Coarser. Repeat until it sings.
03

From cherry to cup, nothing on autopilot.

Most coffee loses its plot somewhere between the farm gate and your grinder. We built the whole chain ourselves — relationships first, contracts second — so every lot lands the way it left.

14partner farms

№ 01

Sourcing

We visit each farm at harvest, cup at origin, and pay above the C-market every single time. Long contracts, fixed premiums, zero middlemen.

№ 02

Cupping & grading

Every lot is triangulated blind three times before it earns a spot on the menu. If it scores under 86, it doesn't ship. Simple.

№ 03

Roasting

Small 12 kg batches on a restored 1968 drum roaster. We log every curve by hand and chase sweetness, not darkness.

№ 04

Resting & packing

Beans degas for 48 hours, then go into one-way-valve bags stamped with the roast date. If it's older than three weeks, we drink it ourselves.

04

Never run dry

Fresh roasts land on your doorstep the week they come off the drum. Pause or cancel any month.
